WHAT THE POOLED VERSION COMPUTED, AND WHY IT WAS SUPERSEDED. One median per
lever level, taken over whatever else that level happened to be measured with.
In this arm that mixes the two levers into each other:

  The swizzle column falls from 0.827 (G=1, n=4) to 0.675 (G=64, n=1), a step of
  0.152. Its G=1 median is two mixtral fits and two qwen2 fits; its G=64 median
  is one qwen2 fit. Most of that step is therefore the model contrast the same
  table reports separately as 0.840 against 0.719. The one cell of (model,
  BLOCK_SIZE_N, BLOCK_M) that holds every swizzle level, (qwen2, 64, 32), moves
  0.724 to 0.675: 0.048. With a single matched cell no paired MDE exists, so
  SURFACE.txt refuses one rather than presenting 0.048 as a measured change.

  The model column reads mixtral 0.840 against qwen2 0.719. Paired over the 4
  cells that hold both models the median difference is 0.130, against a paired
  MDE of 0.040, and it is NOT one-signed: (16, 64, 32) moves +0.054 while the
  other three move -0.282, -0.211 and -0.049. A marginal median cannot show a
  lever that changes sign under it, and this one does.

WHY IT IS KEPT RATHER THAN DELETED. These are the numbers that were published
and that other documents quote. Deleting them would leave those quotations with
no source.
