WHAT THE POOLED VERSION COMPUTED, AND WHY IT WAS SUPERSEDED. One median per
lever level, taken over whatever else that level happened to be measured with.
On this card that gets the SIGN of a lever wrong:

  The swizzle column falls from 0.896 (G=1, n=4) to 0.782 (G=64, n=2), a step of
  0.114 downward. The one cell of (model, BLOCK_SIZE_N, BLOCK_M) that holds
  every swizzle level, (qwen2, 64, 32), moves the other way: 0.651 to 0.679,
  +0.028. The pooled fall is composition. Its G=1 median is two mixtral fits and
  two qwen2 fits while both G=64 fits are qwen2, so what the column reports as
  the swizzle is mostly the footprint contrast underneath it. With a single
  matched cell no paired MDE exists, so SURFACE.txt refuses one rather than
  presenting +0.028 as a measured change.

  The model column reads mixtral 0.866 against qwen2 0.745. Paired over the 4
  cells that hold both models the median difference is 0.070, against a paired
  MDE of 0.040, and it is NOT one-signed: (16, 64, 32) moves +0.092 while the
  other three move -0.367, -0.110 and -0.030. A marginal median cannot show a
  lever that changes sign under it, and this one does.

WHY IT IS KEPT RATHER THAN DELETED. These are the numbers that were published
and that other documents quote. Deleting them would leave those quotations with
no source.
