alpha is the fraction of a weight re-read that MISSES L2. 1.0 means an
extra M-tile costs a full re-read; 0.0 means L2 absorbs it entirely.
A blank alpha means the fit was not identifiable at that BLOCK_M --
fewer than 3 memory-bound treads -- not that it is zero.
REF! is a DIFFERENT state and not a null: that arm's compute
reference was refused on its level, so nothing in it could be
classified at all. Those rows are WITHDRAWN, not uninformative.
