PHYSICAL STATEMENT (why w1 re-reads rise at large P). At G 1 each M-tile reads all P slabs of its expert in column
order, and the expert's next M-tile re-reads each slab P live CTAs later (the only w1 slab re-read at G 1). Between the
two reads the L2 takes in the bytes of every CTA launched in between, from all experts: F = D fbar, fbar = q W / live,
about one slab per CTA at two M-tiles per expert. F grows with P (and with the slab, so with K), from about 3 MiB
(OLMoE) to about 385 MiB (Mixtral-8x22B) against a 60 MiB L2. The laws below differ in two things only: the distance D
of a later-wave re-read (M4's staggered rank distance, or W2's launch-law D_eff = W_c dt / L) and the survival of a
line after F bytes (M4's carried soft law, a step at the 60 MiB L2, or random replacement exp(-F / C)).
