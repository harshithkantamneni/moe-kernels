THRESHOLD-SELECTION RULE (verbatim, Phase 0 section 6):
  For each decode path p (naive, small-batch, block-scan):
  - Take the valid map cells on p in the certified scope (every active expert in one M-tile).
  - For each cell, e = dcommon.e_dec(T_v7, T_meas).
  - e_ub = max(|T_v7/T_lo - 1|, |T_v7/T_hi - 1|), where T_lo, T_hi = T_meas ∓ 2.306 × 1.2533 × s/√n, and s is the sd of the cell's n clean repeats.
  - The candidates are the cells' predicted times. X_p is the smallest candidate X such that every candidate X' ≥ X whose set {T_v7 ≥ X'} holds at least 10 cells has:
    - (i) every e_ub ≤ 6%;
    - (ii) rms e ≤ 2.5%;
    - (iii) |mean e| ≤ 1.0%;
    - and (iv) X_p is the largest of the values this rule gives with each synthetic shape's cells removed in turn.
  - A path with no such X is outside the domain.
  - D = {decode calls whose path has an X_p, whose T_v7 ≥ X_p, and whose every active expert fits one M-tile}.
  
  Slab guard: over family B's cells under a wave, fit resid_us = a + b·W_e_MB + c·1[T=2]. If b's 95% interval excludes 0 and |b|·35 MB ≥ 1 us, D adds W_e ≥ S, with S chosen by the same rule; otherwise D has no slab condition.

HOW read_map.py COMPUTES IT (fixed before any page):
  - valid: the session's power check reads 700 W (d2common.power_check; else nothing is read and D is NOT READ); the page is counted by dcommon.decode_page against this registration's page sha256 and counts sha256 (G5, inst GRR, slip-policy cell, GD4, the r7-median-run3 rebuild and G1); the cell's NATIVE row passes dcommon.row_drop (at least 6 of 9 clean repeats, G2 and GD1, the G4 clock at or over 1705 MHz): an off-lock or lost cell is not valid
  - path: the frozen prediction's path (r3_decode.geometry); scope: no active expert over BLOCK_M rows (d2common.second_tile is None; every registered map cell is in scope by construction, the test is applied anyway)
  - T_v7: the registered prediction in us; T_meas: the rebuilt row's ms_p50 in us (the median of the clean repeats); e = dcommon.e_dec(T_v7, T_meas) (0 at or under 1 us absolute); rms, mean over a set's e
  - s and n: the cell's clean repeats as the rebuild counts them (r7common.clean_r7 at the page's lock: status ok, a time, no lock slip, no run guard), n their number, s their sample standard deviation (n - 1); 2.306 and 1.2533 are constants (t(0.975, 8) and the median's efficiency factor), whatever n is; e_ub carries no 1 us floor (as written: e's floor is dcommon.e_dec's alone); when T_lo <= 0, or n < 2, e_ub is infinite
  - candidates: the distinct T_v7 of the set's cells; a candidate counts only when its set {T_v7 >= X} holds at least 10 cells (a candidate with fewer is neither tested nor eligible as X); X(set) is the smallest counted candidate X such that every counted candidate X' >= X passes (i), (ii) and (iii); none when no counted candidate passes so
  - (iv): the rule above on the path's cells, and again with each synthetic shape's cells removed in turn (the 14 shapes, a shape with no cell on the path giving the full set again); X_p is the largest of these values; a path where any of them is none has no X_p and is outside the domain
  - the slab guard: family B's valid in-scope cells (both paths) with waves under 1 (waves = the larger GEMM's live CTAs over the tile's resident slots, SLOTS); resid_us = T_v7 - T_meas in us; ordinary least squares with an intercept; a regressor constant over the fitted cells (1[T=2] has no family B cell at T 2) is left out; b's 95% interval b +/- t(0.975, n - p) x se(b), p the fitted coefficients; fitted only when n - p >= 1, else NOT FITTED and no slab condition; the guard triggers when the interval excludes 0 and |b| x 35 >= 1 us
  - S when the guard triggers: per path with an X_p, over its cells with T_v7 >= X_p, the same rule with W_e (MB, registered per cell) in place of T_v7: candidates the distinct W_e, counted at 10 or more cells, (i) to (iii), (iv) by shape, S_p the largest; a path with an X_p and no S_p is outside the domain; D then also requires W_e >= S_p
  - D is written per path: X_p (us) or OUTSIDE, S_p (MB) or no slab condition, and the scope condition; read_map.py prints it and writes it to domain.json and domain.txt
