  LADDERS (DESCRIPTIVE; the GEMM ladders on both sides of one wave and within a single wave): per decode tile every ladder's rows by n: n, S, the wave class (sub-wave n <= S, crossing S < n < 2S, multi-wave n >= 2S), b, B, L, Dc, F and X; and which ladders are MIXED (a row with n <= S and a row with n > S: both sides of one wave) and which are SUBWAVE-qualifying (within one wave: two or more sub-wave rows whose n span a factor of 2 or more, every one with b >= 0.5 MB; its smallest n is its few-CTA row). No fit
  SUBWAVE (DESCRIPTIVE; few-CTA long-slab cells within a single wave): rows: the sub-wave rows (n <= S) of every ladder holding two or more. FORMULA per decode tile t: r_cta,t minimises sum ((c0 + max(Dc, F, b / r) - L) / L)^2 over those rows, every c0 free (weighted least squares at each r), r over the registered grid: no ceiling, then 10 x 1.02^j GB/s for j = 0 to 232 (10 to 990 GB/s), ties to the first minimum in that order. r_cta,t is the per-CTA streaming rate a GEMM of few long-slab CTAs runs at when its CTAs cannot fill the DRAM pipe. IDENTIFIED with at least 2 qualifying ladders from at least 2 models (SUBWAVE-qualifying); else NOT IDENTIFIED and not fitted. Reported beside: the binding rows (b / r over max(Dc, F)), the offsets, the relative rms at the fit and with no ceiling
  DRAIN (DESCRIPTIVE; the wave-boundary drain): rows: every row of the tile's MIXED ladders; d = 1 when n > S (the GEMM crosses a wave boundary), else 0. FORMULA per decode tile t: q_t minimises sum ((c0 + max(Dc, F, b / r_cta,t) + d b q - L) / L)^2, every c0 free (closed-form weighted least squares), r_cta,t SUBWAVE's reading on the tile (no ceiling when it reads none or is NOT IDENTIFIED); NO FALLBACK as stated under every reading; a q_t at or under 0 is set to 0 (no drain) and the offsets refitted; r1_t = 1 / q_t GB/s, the rate of the one slab per CTA past the boundary.   W1RATE (DESCRIPTIVE; the per-tile w1 byte-rate pairs): rows: the w1 rows with n > S on each decode tile, past at least one wave boundary (a multi-wave row, n >= 2S, crosses more than one, so the ladder's offset holds a boundary cost only as far as its rows cross the same number of boundaries, and any difference falls into u_t).
