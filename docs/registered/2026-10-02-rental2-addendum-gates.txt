ADDENDUM 2026-10-02 to rental 2's four registrations (docs/registered/2026-10-01-rental2-{knobs,launch2,const,w1floor}-gh200.{json,txt}): how a page that fails a validity gate is counted
fixed: after the rental-2 pages' gate results were seen, before any rental-2 score was computed
the four registrations are unchanged, byte for byte (sha256 in the JSON beside this file); no band, threshold, cell set or stated rule of theirs moves

why: none of the four says how a gate-failed page is counted, and the scorers committed with them read no gate. The 1710 MHz floor captures failed FL1.

where gates are recorded:
  floor capture (r3f-g64[-lock<F>].json, dram_counter_route.r3_floor): `gates` holds FL1 (only under --floor-lock-mhz F) and V1 (launch attribution, with the null kernel); `clock` holds lock_mhz, band_mhz, held, off_lock and the per-GEMM fit; per_gemm[g].sm_clock_mhz is each cell's sm__cycles_elapsed.avg over gpu__time_duration.sum
  byte page (r3c-g<G>.json): `gates` holds V0 to V10 (VALIDITY) and C1 to C6 (CLAIM)
  launch-floor directory (cells.csv, probes, manifests, traces): no validity gate; the registration's P0 is the instrument check
FL1: per GEMM, duration = t0 + cycles / f over its cells; |f - lock| <= 15 MHz (one step), 0 <= t0 <= 50 us, no cell more than 3 steps off the lock's line

gate outcomes known when this was fixed:
  tp8 floor2, tp4 floor, tp2 floor base captures: PASS
  tp8 floor2 1710 capture: FL1 FAIL from a degenerate fit, w1 1690.5 MHz + 3.9 us, w2 1672.6 MHz + 0.6 us (a low f traded against a small t0); no evidence of an off-lock run
  tp4 floor 1710 capture: FL1 FAIL, fitted w1 1707.3 MHz, w2 1695.6 MHz, one cell (n=1 w2) at 1785 MHz
  tp2 floor 1710 capture: FL1 FAIL, fitted w1 1710.4 MHz, w2 1703.2 MHz, n=1 w2 at 1773 MHz
  tp4 floor1005 capture: PASS
  every other rental-2 page: not seen
clock readings known: the null kernel's sm_clock_mhz reads the same 1684 to 1688 MHz median on the tp8, tp4 and tp2 1710 captures (tp4's and tp2's fits sit on the lock) and 991 MHz at the 1005 lock, a uniform -1.4% bias of that reading, not an off-lock run (the published sessions show the same: every FL1 / V10 fail with t0 under about 5 us reads a low f while the L2-clock fit and the cell-matched base/lock ratio put the SM clock on the lock; scratchpad gate-audit/AUDIT.md)

the rule:
  1. Every page counts at the clock its own cells measured, never the lock asked, and no absolute reading of a field with a known bias enters a verdict: sm_clock_mhz reads about 1.4% low uniformly, so it may enter only as a ratio of two captures, where the bias cancels. Clock source per scorer: part 3, K1, K2 and K4 are in SM cycles and read no clock; K3's rho_f = f_1005 / f_1710 is a ratio of the two captures' median per-cell sm_clock_mhz (the bias cancels); each capture's clock_mhz is printed only. Part 4 is in SM cycles and reads no clock. Part 1 reads bytes and occupancy, clock-free. Part 2 compares host-timed milliseconds with the registered C_reg and F and reads no clock field; repricing C_reg would change a registered prediction, which this addendum does not do. No scorer used a nominal lock clock or an absolute measured clock in a verdict.
  2. In a floor capture taken under a lock, a (cell, GEMM) whose own sm_clock_mhz is above the capture's lock by more than one step (15 MHz) is dropped, in both views, and recorded. A clock above the lock cannot be run; such readings come only from short GEMMs, where ncu's fixed duration offset dominates cycles over duration. Base-clock captures carry no lock and drop nothing; byte pages are not touched (their scores read no clock). This test reads sm_clock_mhz absolutely; its known bias is downward, so it can only leave an above-lock cell in, never drop one that ran at the lock, and it decides which cells are kept, not any verdict's value.
  3. Every verdict is computed twice: ALL (every page, gate-failed ones included, under rules 1 and 2) and CLEAN (every page that failed a validity gate excluded; for a floor capture under a lock, rule 5's check in place of FL1). This covers each K and the identification (part 3), the families, theta call, w2 offset and co-primary per-cell test (part 4), every part-1 test, the partition cross-check and the T5 candidates, and every P of part 2. The registered verdict is the common one when ALL and CLEAN agree, INCONCLUSIVE when they disagree, and NOT SCORED when CLEAN has no data and ALL does; ALL's verdict is then printed beside it, labelled as reading only gate-failed pages: the label means that verdict exists only because gate-failed pages were read (K3, for one, reads the 1005 capture beside a 1710 one).
  4. A page failing any validity gate other than FL1 is handled by the same rule. A gate fails when its verdict is not PASS (FAIL or REFUSE); CLAIM gates (C1 to C6) are findings and gate nothing. Exception: a page whose V1 (launch attribution) failed is unusable in both views.
  5. For a floor capture taken under a lock, CLEAN does not use FL1: it uses V1 PASS and a lock-in-force check from an independent clock, the median over the capture's cells of the null kernel's sm_clock_mhz within 3% of the lock (its bias is a uniform -1.4%; 3% passes a held lock and catches one not in force, as the 2026-09-28 8x22B floor at about +5.6%). A capture with no null kernel falls back to FL1. FL1 is still recorded and printed per page. This check was chosen after the rental-2 pages' gate outcomes and null-kernel clock medians were seen (tp8, tp4, tp2 1710 medians 1684, 1687, 1688 MHz; tp4 1005 median 991 MHz) and before any score was computed. FL1 is set aside because of the gate audit of the published pre-rental-2 data (scratchpad gate-audit/AUDIT.md): whenever its fitted t0 is small its fitted f reads low, a degenerate f / t0 fit, while cell-matched lock / base ratios and the L2 counter's clock show the locks held; the only real off-lock floor (8x22B, the lock not in force) was a gross error.
  kept as registered: in part 1 a V6 failure flags the page and its verdicts read INCONCLUSIVE in both views, and V10 gates no part-1 score (bytes are clock-free); neither is a CLEAN exclusion there.

consequence of the known outcomes, stated before any score: by the null-kernel medians every 1710 capture and the 1005 capture pass the lock check, so CLEAN keeps them; part 3 and part 4's lock1710 view are scored on both views by rule 3.

scorer: scripts/scoring/rental2/common.py (failed_gates, drop_above_lock, View, combine_verdict, combine, two_views), used by every score_*.py; tested on synthetic pages only (tests/test_scoring_rental2.py)
